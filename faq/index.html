Frequently Asked Questions

Answers before your service call.

Helpful information about coverage, equipment, and what to expect.

Do you provide Biodex service outside Florida?

Yes. Specialized Biodex service may be scheduled nationwide depending on the equipment, location, scope, and availability.

Which Biodex systems do you service?

Biodex physical medicine equipment, including System 2, 3 and 4 dynamometers, Balance System SD and BioSway, Gait Trainer 3, NxStep, FreeStep, BioStep 2 and the Upper Body Cycle. Share your model for a feasibility assessment, since parts availability varies on older units.

Are you the manufacturer?

No. Precision MedTech Services is an independent service provider. Factory training and prior manufacturer experience do not imply current manufacturer endorsement.

Who do you serve?

Professional sports teams (MLB and NFL), universities and athletic departments, hospitals and health systems, PT clinics and wellness centers, NASA and research facilities, and commercial gyms and strength facilities.

Do you service other rehabilitation equipment?

Yes. Florida service includes selected treadmills, steppers, cycles, strength equipment, therapy tables, and other rehabilitation devices.

Can you guarantee parts for older systems?

No. Parts availability varies. We discuss service feasibility and limitations before making commitments.

What information should I send?

The equipment model and serial number, a description of symptoms, any error codes, your facility location, and the best contact information.

Need dependable equipment service?

Share your equipment model, location and service needs. We’ll help you determine the next step.
