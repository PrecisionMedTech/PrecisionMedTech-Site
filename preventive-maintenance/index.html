Proactive Equipment Care

Preventive maintenance that helps protect uptime.

Keep rehabilitation equipment safe, dependable, and ready when therapists and patients need it. Biodex PM nationwide; PT and fitness equipment statewide across Florida.

Why It Matters

Most expensive repairs start as small problems.

A loose cable strain relief, a lock that’s been overtightened for months, a belt that slips once in a while. None of these stop a clinic on their own. Left alone, they turn into failed boards, broken welds and equipment that’s out of service for weeks.

Preventive maintenance catches those issues while they’re still small and inexpensive. It also gives you a documented service history, which matters when equipment is used with patients every day. Equipment that is still running but starting to behave inconsistently is the riskiest kind, because staff keep using it. Read more in The Unreliable Zone.

A Practical Approach

Routine service with clear documentation.

01

Visual and mechanical inspection

Review wear, fasteners, cables, locks, pedals, belts, upholstery and frame condition, plus any relevant safety concerns.

02

Functional checks

Check equipment operation and performance as applicable to each device: drives, controls, displays, safety stops and moving assemblies.

03

Documented findings

A written summary of maintenance performed, issues found, and recommended next steps, so you have a clear record for every unit.

Scope and test procedures depend on the equipment model and service agreement.

Equipment Covered

What a PM visit covers.

01

Biodex & SMTI (nationwide)

Isokinetic dynamometers (System 2, 3 and 4 Pro), Balance System SD, BioSway, Gait Trainer 3, NxStep, BioStep 2, UBE and other Biodex physical medicine equipment. Checks include signal cables and strain reliefs, tilt and rotation locks, trolley and seat mechanisms, and system operation.

04

Tables, bars & strength (Florida)

Treatment tables, parallel bars, BariHab and PMX equipment. Checks include lift and height mechanisms, frames, fasteners and stability.

Common Questions

Preventive maintenance FAQ

How often should rehab equipment get preventive maintenance?

Most facilities schedule it once or twice a year. Busy clinics, equipment used every day, and older units usually benefit from semi-annual visits. The right interval depends on the equipment and how hard it works.

Do you calibrate equipment during a PM visit?

PM visits include inspection and functional checks of how the equipment operates. Findings are documented in a written report. Ask about the scope for your specific equipment when you request service.

Can you cover several brands in one visit?

Yes. One visit can cover Biodex, NuStep, SciFit, Landice, PMX, Matrix, BariHab, treatment tables and parallel bars, which saves scheduling multiple vendors.

Do you offer service agreements?

Yes. A preventive maintenance agreement puts your equipment on a regular schedule with predictable costs. Contact us with your equipment list for a quote.
