What I Service

Biodex System 4, System 3 and System 2 problems I repair.

Knowing these dynamometers from the inside means finding the actual fault first, instead of replacing parts or taking apart major assemblies that never needed it.

01

Motor & drive

Motor and drive problems, and position or speed irregularities during testing or exercise.

02

Cables & mechanics

Damaged signal cables at the gimbal post, and wear in the tilt, rotation and trolley mechanisms.

03

Electronics & communication

Board-level faults, power issues, and communication problems between the computer and the dynamometer, including on older platforms many facilities still depend on.

Calibration & Preventive Maintenance

Accurate data depends on a calibrated system.

Clinicians make decisions from the torque, position and speed data an isokinetic system reports. Calibration and preventive maintenance help keep that data trustworthy and catch wear before it turns into a breakdown.

Equipment is restored to the manufacturer’s specifications and never modified, and every visit ends with a documented service record. See Preventive Maintenance for how a maintenance plan works.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you calibrate Biodex System 4 dynamometers?

Yes. I calibrate Biodex isokinetic systems as part of repair and preventive maintenance visits, and every visit ends with a documented service record.

Can you still repair an older System 2 or System 3?

Often, yes. Many facilities still rely on legacy Biodex systems. Some parts are no longer produced, so I assess the fault and parts availability before promising a repair.

Do you service this equipment outside Florida?

Yes. Biodex service is available nationwide. On-site visits outside Florida are scheduled based on location, scope and availability, and many issues can be narrowed down by phone or email first.

What should I have ready when I call?

The model, serial number, the symptoms, any error messages, when the problem started and your facility location. Photos of the equipment and the screen help a lot.

Are you affiliated with Biodex?

No. Precision MedTech Services is an independent service organization. I spent 30 years as a Biodex field service engineer, but no manufacturer endorsement is implied.
