Your Rights

Your equipment. Your patients. Your decision.

Healthcare facilities have full authority to select the qualified service provider of their choice for the physical therapy and Biodex equipment they own, regardless of manufacturer affiliation.

Precision MedTech Services supports that right and ensures you maintain full control over your maintenance budget, scheduling, and service standards.

Even when replacement parts must be sourced through the manufacturer or distributor, facilities retain the freedom to choose who performs the repair. Precision MedTech Services can assist in confirming part needs and will provide expert installation once those parts arrive onsite — anywhere in the country for Biodex/SMTI equipment, and statewide across Florida for PT and fitness equipment.
