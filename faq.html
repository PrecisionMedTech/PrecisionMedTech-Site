Frequently Asked Questions

Do you repair physical therapy equipment anywhere in Florida?

Yes. We service all of Florida, including Orlando, Miami, Tampa, Jacksonville, Gainesville, Tallahassee, Naples, Sarasota, Pensacola, and every surrounding area.

Do you repair Biodex equipment?

Absolutely. We have over 30 years as a Biodex-certified technician, specializing in System 3 & 4, gait and balance systems, and all Biodex rehab equipment.

Do you service rehab treadmills and weight-stack machines?

Yes. We repair treadmills, weight-stack systems, cable machines, gait trainers, dynamometers, and all major rehab and therapy equipment.

Do you offer preventative maintenance?

Yes. Annual PMs help extend equipment life, reduce downtime, and ensure accurate performance.

Can most PT equipment be repaired without parts?

Often, yes. Many common failures can be repaired on-site without parts, saving clinics time and money.

Do you work with university athletics and sports-medicine programs?

Yes. We service rehab and performance equipment for sports teams, universities, pro/college athletics, and high-performance training centers.

How quickly can you respond?

We offer fast statewide response based on scheduling and region. Most clinics receive service much faster than OEM providers.
