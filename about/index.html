Our Story

Why I started Precision MedTech Services.

Chris Senzamici, owner and technician.

Built On Purpose

Returning to what I’m truly passionate about.

After more than 35 years in the field, I realized I wanted to return to what I was truly passionate about — servicing physical therapy and rehabilitation equipment. Throughout my career, I saw firsthand the real-time impact that properly functioning equipment has on patient outcomes.

When a physical therapist has reliable, accurate, and fully operational equipment, the results can be life-changing. I’ve seen adults regain mobility, children build strength and confidence, and senior citizens continue living active, independent lives.

Being part of that process — even behind the scenes — has always meant something to me.

A Career Built On Experience And Purpose

Why I opened Precision MedTech Services.

It’s why I returned to this work. I wanted to continue supporting the therapists and facilities who dedicate their lives to helping others — and, as the only factory-trained independent technician in the country for Biodex/SMTI equipment, to bring that same standard of care to clinics and hospitals nationwide.

I started in R&D engineering at Cybex, then spent three decades at Biodex servicing physical therapy, radiology and nuclear medicine equipment, followed by years at Stryker supporting operating-room equipment. Over 35 years of specialized technical experience has shown me every side of the healthcare environment. From elite sports medicine programs to the operating room, I’ve learned that the reliability of your technology directly impacts the quality of your patient care.

I founded Precision MedTech Services to bring this national-level expertise directly to the facilities that need it — nationwide Biodex repair, diagnostics, and preventive maintenance, plus statewide PT and fitness equipment service across Florida. My mission is to ensure your clinic has the fast, accurate, and reliable support it needs to help people recover and get their lives back.
