Our Story

Why I Started Precision MedTech Services, LLC

After more than 35 years in the field, I realized I wanted to return to what I was truly passionate about — servicing physical therapy and rehabilitation equipment. Throughout my career, I saw firsthand the real-time impact that properly functioning equipment has on patient outcomes.

When a physical therapist has reliable, accurate, and fully operational equipment, the results can be life-changing. I've seen adults regain mobility, children build strength and confidence, and senior citizens continue living active, independent lives.

Being part of that process — even behind the scenes — has always meant something to me.

It's why I returned to this work and why I opened Precision MedTech Services. I wanted to continue supporting the therapists and facilities who dedicate their lives to helping others.

A Career Built on Experience and Purpose

With over 35 years of specialized technical experience — including three decades as a senior Biodex technician and five years in field service for high-stakes surgical equipment — I have seen every side of the healthcare environment. From elite sports medicine programs to the operating room, I've learned that the reliability of your technology directly impacts the quality of your patient care.

I founded Precision MedTech Services to bring this national-level expertise home to Florida. I witnessed day after day how essential well-maintained equipment is to a patient's journey; now, my mission is to ensure your clinic has the fast, accurate, and reliable support it needs to help people recover and get their lives back.

Who We Serve

  • Physical Therapy & Rehabilitation Hospitals
  • Sports Medicine & Performance Programs
  • University Athletic Departments
  • Commercial Gyms and Strength Facilities

Whether you are helping seniors maintain mobility or returning professional athletes to the field, my goal is the same — to ensure your physical therapy equipment is safe, precise, and performing at its best.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: use my experience, passion, and technical skill to keep your equipment operating safely and accurately so you can continue changing lives every day.

Precision MedTech Services exists because I believe in what you do — and I'm honored to support the work of every therapist, trainer, and clinician who depends on the equipment I service.
